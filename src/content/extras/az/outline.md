## Observables

### Red rock Canyon National Conservation Area

1. There are 60 total posts on the Kingfisher Bridge  
2. The Schuerman Homestead was built in 1931 by Fritz Schuerman for his wife Dolly and children Jane \+ Dale. Jack \+ Helen Frye bought it in 1942 and rename it "Willow House". You can read this on a sign there.  
3. The fence at the Apache Fire area was built by Buffalo Fence out of Flagstaff, AZ – Number is 526-0566
