# Twelve Longer Ones

Longer setups, harder solves. Several are old chestnuts, which is fine.
Read one out, let the car fight about it. Answers in ANSWERS-3.md.

**1.** A man lives on the tenth floor. Every morning he takes the elevator
down to the lobby and goes to work. Every evening he takes the elevator back
up to the seventh floor and walks the last three flights. Except on rainy
days, when he rides all the way to ten. And except when he is not alone in
the elevator, when he also rides all the way to ten. Why?

**2.** You reach two doors. One leads out, one does not. Each has a guard.
One guard always tells the truth and one always lies, and you have no idea
which is which. You may ask one question, to one guard. What do you ask?

**3.** A dealer offers a collector a bronze coin. It is beautifully made, and
stamped clearly on the face is the date 200 BC. The collector hands it back
and says it is a fake. He has not tested the metal or examined the wear. How
does he know?

**4.** You have two lengths of rope and a lighter. Each rope takes exactly one
hour to burn end to end, but they burn unevenly, so half a rope is not half an
hour. You cannot measure them, cut them to length, or mark them. Measure out
exactly forty-five minutes.

**5.** A game show puts a car behind one of three doors and a goat behind the
other two. You pick door 1. The host, who knows where the car is, opens door 3
and shows you a goat. He offers to let you switch to door 2. Should you?

**6.** Three switches at the bottom of the stairs. One of them controls a
single light bulb in the attic, and you cannot see the bulb from the
switches. You may flip the switches as much as you like, but you may climb the
stairs only once. Which switch is it?

**7.** An old man is dying and calls in his two sons. He tells them to race
their camels to the far city, and that the son whose camel arrives last
inherits everything. The two sons wander the desert for weeks, each refusing
to go faster than the other. Finally they visit a wise woman. She says two
words to them. They leap onto the camels and race at full speed toward the
city. What did she say?

**8.** A monk leaves the monastery at dawn and walks a narrow mountain path to
the temple at the summit, arriving at dusk. He rests overnight. At dawn the
next morning he starts back down the same path and reaches the monastery at
dusk. He walked at whatever speed he felt like on both days, stopping and
starting as he pleased. Prove there is a spot on the path he occupied at
exactly the same time of day on both trips.

**9.** A prisoner is told he may make one statement. If the statement is true
he will be hanged. If it is false he will be shot. He makes a statement and
they have to let him go. What did he say?

**10.** Four people must cross a bridge at night. They have one flashlight and
the bridge holds at most two people at a time. Whoever crosses must carry the
flashlight, so someone has to walk it back. The four of them cross at
different speeds: one minute, two minutes, five minutes, and ten minutes. A
pair crosses at the slower one's pace. Get all four across in seventeen
minutes.

**11.** A clock takes six seconds to strike six o'clock, measured from the
first strike to the last. How long does it take to strike twelve?

**12.** Three people stand in a line, all facing forward, so the back one sees
the two in front, the middle one sees the front one, and the front one sees
nobody. Each has a hat placed on their head, drawn from a bag known to hold
three white hats and two black hats. In fact all three get white. Nobody can
see their own hat. The back one is asked what color his hat is; he says he
does not know. The middle one is asked; he does not know either. The front
one, who has seen nothing at all, says "mine is white." How?
