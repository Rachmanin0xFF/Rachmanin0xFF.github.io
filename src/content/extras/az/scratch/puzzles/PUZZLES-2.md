# Twelve More

A step up. Same rules: read one out, let people argue, move on.
Answers in ANSWERS-2.md.

**1.** A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs one dollar more
than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

**2.** Romeo and Juliet are found dead on the floor in a puddle of water with
broken glass nearby. Nobody hurt them. What happened?

**3.** Why are these numbers in this order?

    8, 5, 4, 9, 1, 7, 6, 10, 3, 2

**4.** Its first two letters are a man, its first three a woman, its first
four a great man, and the whole thing a great woman. What is the word?

**5.** What five-letter word sounds exactly the same after you remove four of
its letters?

**6.** What does this say?

    ZERO
    ----
    B.S.
    M.A.
    Ph.D.

**7.** A man walks into a bar and asks for a glass of water. The bartender
pulls out a gun and points it at him. The man says thank you and leaves.
Why?

**8.** You are in a race. You pass the person in second place. What place are
you in now?

**9.** A man builds a house with four walls. Every wall faces south. A bear
walks past the window. What color is the bear?

**10.** A patch of lily pads doubles in size every day. On day 48 it covers
the whole lake. On what day did it cover half the lake?

**11.** What comes next?

    1
    11
    21
    1211
    111221

**12.** What word in the English language is always spelled incorrectly?
