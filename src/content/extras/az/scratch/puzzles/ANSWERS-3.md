# Answers, Round Three

1. **He is short.** He can reach the button for seven but not for ten. An
   umbrella gives him the extra reach, and a fellow passenger will press it
   for him.

2. **Ask either guard: "If I asked the other guard which door leads out, what
   would he say?"** Then take the other door. The truth teller reports the
   liar's lie; the liar lies about the truth teller's truth. Either way you
   get the wrong door, reliably.

3. **Nobody in 200 BC knew that Christ was coming.** The dating system did not
   exist yet, so no coin was stamped that way at the time.

4. **Forty-five minutes.** Light rope A at both ends and rope B at one end, all
   at the same moment. Rope A burns out after thirty minutes. At that instant,
   light rope B's other end. Rope B had thirty minutes left, so burning from
   both ends it finishes in fifteen. Thirty plus fifteen.

5. **Switch.** Your first pick was right one time in three, and that has not
   changed. So two times in three the car is behind the other unopened door.
   The host was never going to open the car, which is what makes his choice
   informative.

6. **Turn switch 1 on and leave it for ten minutes. Turn it off, turn switch 2
   on, and go up.** Bulb lit, it is switch 2. Bulb dark but warm, switch 1.
   Bulb dark and cold, switch 3.

7. **"Switch camels."** Each brother now rides the other's camel, so each
   wants the animal underneath him to arrive first.

8. **Imagine a second monk walking down while the first walks up, on the same
   day.** They are on the same path moving toward each other, so they must
   pass somewhere. That meeting point and time is the answer. It does not
   matter how either of them paced himself.

9. **"I will be shot."** If it is true, they must hang him, which makes it
   false. If it is false, they must shoot him, which makes it true. Neither
   punishment can be carried out.

10. **17 minutes.** The 1 and 2 cross together (2). The 1 comes back (1). The
    5 and 10 cross together (10). The 2 comes back (2). The 1 and 2 cross
    (2). Total 2 + 1 + 10 + 2 + 2 = 17. The trick is sending the two slowest
    together, so the 10 costs you nothing extra.

11. **13.2 seconds.** Six strikes have five gaps between them, so a gap is 1.2
    seconds. Twelve strikes have eleven gaps. Not twelve seconds.

12. **The front one reasons from the other two men's silence.** If the front
    two hats were both black, the man at the back would know his own was
    white, since there are only two black hats. He did not know, so the front
    two are not both black. The middle man works that out too, so if the front
    hat were black, the middle man would know his own was white. He did not
    know either. Therefore the front hat is white.
