# Answers, Round Two

1. **Five cents.** The bat is $1.05. Almost everyone says ten cents, which
   would make the bat $1.00 and the total $1.10 wrong by a dime.

2. **They are fish.** The bowl got knocked over.

3. **Alphabetical order.** Eight, five, four, nine, one, seven, six, ten,
   three, two.

4. **HEROINE.** He, her, hero, heroine.

5. **QUEUE.** Drop the last four letters and it is still pronounced "Q."

6. **THREE DEGREES BELOW ZERO.** Three academic degrees, under the word zero.

7. **He had the hiccups.** The water was the cure he asked for. The scare
   worked faster.

8. **Second.** You took their place. You did not take the leader's.

9. **White.** Four walls all facing south puts the house at the North Pole,
   so it is a polar bear.

10. **Day 47.** Doubling means the last day is the one that does half the
    work.

11. **312211.** Read each line out loud as a description of the line above
    it. 1211 is "one one, one two, two ones," which gives 111221. That line
    is "three ones, two twos, one one."

12. **INCORRECTLY.**
