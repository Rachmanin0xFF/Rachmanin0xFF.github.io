# Ten Puzzles

Read one out loud, let people argue, move on. Answers in ANSWERS.md.

**1.** What is the next letter in this sequence?

    O, T, T, F, F, S, S, E, ?

**2.** What do these four words have in common?

    SCOLD    CHEAT    SWARM    BRAIN

**3.** A man pushes his car until he reaches a hotel, then tells the owner
he is bankrupt. Why?

**4.** Add a single line to make this true. You may not touch the equals sign.

    5 + 5 + 5 = 550

**5.** What does this say?

    GROUND
    FEET FEET FEET FEET FEET FEET

**6.** What occurs once in a minute, twice in a moment, but never in a
thousand years?

**7.** What English word becomes shorter when you add two letters to it?

**8.** A cowboy rides into town on Friday. He stays three days and rides out
on Friday. How?

**9.** Name three days in a row without using the words Monday, Tuesday,
Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.

**10.** Forward I am heavy. Backward I am not. What am I?
