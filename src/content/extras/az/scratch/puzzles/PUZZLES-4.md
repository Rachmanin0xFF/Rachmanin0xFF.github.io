# Twelve Less Familiar

Same length and difficulty as round three, but off the beaten track. Answers
in ANSWERS-4.md.

**1.** Every morning a man wheels a barrow of sand up to the border crossing.
The guard is certain he is smuggling something, so he empties the barrow and
sifts the sand, every single day, for two years. He never finds anything, and
the man is never charged. The guard retires, runs into the man in a bar, and
says he will not report it now, he just has to know. What was the man
smuggling?

**2.** You are alone in the middle of a long, dry island covered in grass,
running east to west. A strong wind is blowing from the west, and the western
end of the island is on fire. The fire is moving toward you at walking pace
and the island is too long to outrun it. The water on all sides is too rough
to swim and there is nothing to float on. You have a box of matches. How do
you live?

**3.** A spy is watching the door of an enemy safehouse, trying to learn the
password. A man knocks. The guard says "twelve." The man says "six," and is
let in. Another man knocks. The guard says "six." The man says "three," and is
let in. The spy thinks he has it, walks up, and knocks. The guard says "ten."
The spy says "five," and is shot. What should he have said?

**4.** Three people are in a duel and they shoot in turn, over and over, until
one is left. You are the worst shot and hit one time in three. The second hits
two times in three. The third never misses. Out of fairness you shoot first,
then the second, then the third, skipping anyone already dead. Everyone plays
to win. Where do you aim your first shot?

**5.** You take two medicines daily, pill A and pill B, and you must take
exactly one of each. Taking two of the same one will kill you. This morning
you tipped one A into your palm, then fumbled the other bottle and two B's
came out. All three pills are identical in size, color, weight, and smell.
They cost a fortune and you cannot throw them away. How do you take your dose
today?

**6.** On a table in a pitch-black room are one hundred coins. Exactly ten of
them are lying heads up and the rest are tails up, but you cannot see them and
you cannot tell by touch which way any coin is facing. You may move coins and
you may flip them over. Divide them into two groups that contain the same
number of heads.

**7.** You have a glass of wine and a glass of water, filled to exactly the
same level. Take a spoonful of wine, stir it into the water, and stir
thoroughly. Now take a spoonful of that mixture and stir it back into the
wine. Is there more wine in the water glass, or more water in the wine glass?

**8.** A moneylender holds a debt over a merchant and wants to marry his
daughter. He proposes a wager: he will put two stones in a bag, one black and
one white, and the daughter will draw one. White cancels the debt and she goes
free. Black cancels the debt and she marries him. They meet on a gravel path
to do it, and the daughter sees him pick up two black stones and drop them in
the bag. She cannot accuse him without calling him a cheat in front of her
father's creditors, which would end the deal and ruin her father. She draws.
She goes free, the debt is cancelled, and nobody is accused of anything. How?

**9.** A man walked a tightrope between two towers every night for eleven
years, while a band played below. He had done it in wind and in rain and
never come close to falling. One night the band stopped playing partway
through and he fell to his death. Why?

**10.** A census taker knocks and asks a woman how many children she has. She
says three daughters. He asks their ages. She says the product of their ages
is 36, and the sum of their ages is the number on the house next door. He
walks over, looks at the house, comes back, and says he still cannot work it
out. She says "my eldest plays the piano." He thanks her and writes down three
numbers. What are they?

**11.** You have ten sacks of gold coins. Every genuine coin weighs ten grams.
One sack is entirely counterfeit and every coin in it weighs nine grams. The
sacks look identical and you may take out as many coins as you like. You have
a digital scale that reads exact weight, and you are allowed to use it once.
Which sack is the fake?

**12.** A man dies and leaves seventeen camels to his three sons. The will
says the eldest gets one half of the camels, the middle son one third, and the
youngest one ninth. Seventeen divides by none of these, and the sons will not
cut a camel in pieces. They are still arguing when a traveller rides up on her
own camel, listens to the problem, and settles it in about a minute, to
everyone's satisfaction and at no cost to herself. How?
