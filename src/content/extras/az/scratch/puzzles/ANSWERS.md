# Answers

1. **N.** They are the first letters of One, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six,
   Seven, Eight, Nine.

2. **WEATHER.** Drop the first letter of each: cold, heat, warm, rain.

3. **MONOPOLY.** He is playing a board game.

4. **FOUR.** Turn the first plus sign into a 4, giving 545 + 5 = 550.

5. **SIX FEET UNDER.** Six feet sit under the ground.

6. **The letter M.** One M in "minute," two in "moment," none in "a thousand
   years."

7. **SHORT.** Add E and R and you get shorter.

8. **His horse is named Friday.**

9. **Yesterday, today, tomorrow.**

10. **TON.** Backward it spells "not."
